# Video Conference System (화상회의 시스템)

A video conference system is installed for various meetings (Board of Education, directors meetings, team leader meetings, etc.).

##### Installation Locations

**Humanities and Social Sciences Campus**

- Conference Room 1 — 600th Anniversary Hall, 3F
- Seminar Room 1 — 600th Anniversary Hall, 6F

**Natural Sciences Campus**

- Conference Room 1 — Engineering Building 2, 2F
- Conference Room 2 — Engineering Building 2, 2F

##### Users and Reservations

- Users: Members of Sungkyunkwan University
- To reserve a conference room, contact the meeting manager of the Information and Communications Team: 031-299-6119 (ext. 6119)
