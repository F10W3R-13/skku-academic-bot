# Dasomi (다소미 봉사단)

"Dasomi" is an official student volunteer group in Sungkyunkwan University's Student Affairs Division. The name derives from the Korean word "Dasom" meaning love. Since its foundation in **1996**, Dasomi has worked on the idea that services can be provided even in very small parts of our lives, under the principle of "I can help others myself".

##### On-Campus Activities

- Coordinates official SKKU events (Injajeon 인자전, etc.) to ensure they run smoothly and encourage student participation
- During festivals, opens a weekly booth to sell items that help the socially disadvantaged and promote related campaigns
- Working in farming villages, providing education services for local children's centers, conducting mentoring camps for college students under the Korean Scholarship Foundation
- Student-autonomous volunteer work such as the "Thank You" project — sending appreciation letters and gifts to the school's environment cleaners and traffic agents

##### Off-Campus Activities

Regular services are performed more than once a week in conjunction with **Margaret's Children's Center** and the **Jongno General Social Welfare Center**, promoting the improvement of academic abilities and emotional development of elementary and middle school students. Other activities include providing heating briquettes, assistance at Kimchi cultural events, and mural services.
