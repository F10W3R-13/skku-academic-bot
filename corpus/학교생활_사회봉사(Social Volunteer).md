# Social Volunteer (사회봉사 UR1004)

To realize SKKU's founding philosophy of 修己治人 (su-gyi-chi-in), the University runs an online social service website called **UR1004** with university-supervised social service programs. These are part of the **"Character Quality" (인성품) requirement of SKKU's Three-Quality System (삼품제 / Sam Poom Jae: Character Quality, Global Quality, Information Quality)**.

UR1004 is operated by the SKKU Social Service Team and connects students who want volunteer experience with organizations.

##### Roles of UR1004

- Provision of practical and detailed information about social services in general
- Organization of applicants
- Management and supply of social service activities
- Establishment and coordination of new strategies for social services
- Provision of counseling on issues regarding social service activities

##### Samsung Seoul Hospital Associated Volunteer Work

Volunteer programs at Samsung Seoul Hospital, organized through an agreement between SKKU's Social Service Team and Samsung Medical Center in Gangnam. The volunteer team is organized and operated by students selected through a recruitment process.

Main programs:

- Hospital Ward Support (conversations with elderly patients, exercise aid for surgical patients, etc.)
- Mobile Library Support (library services in patients' rooms)
- Visitor Support at the Main Building Entrance (escort patients in wheelchairs)
- Outpatient Work Support (questionnaire & survey assistance, data input, escorting patients, transferring charts, emergency room assistance, etc.)

Application details are announced on the SKKU website at the beginning of a semester.

##### Education Office Associated Mentoring Volunteer Work

Supported by Korea's Ministry of Education, this program connects university student mentors to youths from low-income families for individualized learning and character development. SKKU selects and sends volunteer student mentors based on its agreement with the Gangnam District Office of Education and the Gyeonggi Provincial Office of Education.

Main activities:

- Teaching and child care assistance for elementary school students
- Korean language classes for children of foreign workers
- Teaching for underachieving students
- Instructors or instructor assistants in skills and aptitudes programs
- Career counseling, etc.

Details announced on the SKKU website at the beginning of a semester.

##### Countryside Volunteering (농촌봉사활동)

SKKU and Chungju City concluded the 'Farm Love' agreement in April 2006 and began offering countryside volunteer programs for different seasons (spring, summer, fall).

**Summer countryside volunteering is scheduled for five nights and six days, and volunteers are acknowledged for 40 hours of service in the Character Quality (인성품) requirement.**

Main activities: helping out with farm work; offering academic support for children; apple picking and making flower roads; other volunteer activities as requested by the villages.

Details announced on the SKKU website during recruitment periods.
