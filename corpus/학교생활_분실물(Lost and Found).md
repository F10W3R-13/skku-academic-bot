# Lost and Found (분실물)

##### Lost Item Report

- Students must first go to Lost & Found locations and check if their items have been found and where they are being held to retrieve the item.
- If a lost item is not registered at a Lost & Found location, go to the Student Support Team and submit a self-report.

##### Found Item Report

- If a student finds a lost item, they must turn the item in to the security office of the building or the Student Support Team.
- If a student does not retrieve their item even after making contact to claim ownership, the item will be disposed of at the discretion of SKKU 100 days after posting a notification for retrieval on SKKU's newspaper or notice board.
- The relevant departments (department office, Student Support Team) post information about found items on SKKU's notice boards. If the items are not retrieved after a reasonable amount of time, the department contacts the owners individually.

##### Lost & Found Locations

- Security office of each building, or the Student Support Team
- Humanities and Social Sciences Campus: 1F, 600th Anniversary Hall, 02-760-1077
