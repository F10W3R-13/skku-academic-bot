# Human Rights Center (인권센터)

The Human Rights Center was established to make Sungkyunkwan University a community where all members have mutual care and respect for one another.

- Homepage: http://helper.skku.edu/

##### Human Rights Center

The Human Rights Center also focuses on educating and fostering a culture of respect at SKKU.
In addition to the Sexual Assault Counselling Service, the center conducts specialized training programs for each department to raise awareness on a wide range of human rights issues.

- Office Hours: 09:00 ~ 17:30 (Monday~Friday)
