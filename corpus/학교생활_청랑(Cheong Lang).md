# Cheong Lang / 청랑 (Blue Wave)

Chung Lang (Blue Wave, 청랑) is a student club that inherits the old tradition of Sungkyunkwan Confucian scholars, called **Yusaeng (유생)** in the Joseon Dynasty.

Chung Lang aims to create a unique campus culture by organizing events that interpret traditional elements of the old Sungkyunkwan in a modern way, so that all SKKU students can enjoy and take pride in the history of the University. The club studies the history of the old Confucian scholars and revives some of the old Sungkyunkwan ceremonies based on historical records with a modern touch.

##### Shin Bhang Rye (신방례)

Shin Bhang Rye was a rite of passage and welcome event for all newly admitted Yusaeng who passed the national exam at Sungkyunkwan in the Joseon Dynasty. It was organized by senior Yusaeng: the freshmen were given team tasks, and shared food and snacks with the senior students.

Chung Lang's Shin Bhang Rye is held **every year in early March**. It begins with an official matriculation ceremony where freshmen visit **Munmyo (문묘)**, the shrine of the great Confucian scholars, in traditional costumes. Afterward, participants share food and snacks, play quiz games, and join various programs to help freshmen learn about Sungkyunkwan's history. At the end, all students are given souvenirs, a certificate, and **Hopae (호패)**, personal identifications used in the Joseon Dynasty.

##### Gohanora (고하노라)

Gohanora came from **Yuso (유소)**, an event organized by Yusaeng in the Joseon Dynasty. Yuso means 'Confucian scholars' petition to the King'. Yusaeng were considered future elite officials; they held a meeting, wrote a joint petition to the King, and after all signed it, marched to the King's Palace to deliver their proposals. Joseon history says Yusaeng's rights to petition were protected by the King.

On the day of Gohanora **in September**, all participants wear traditional Yusaeng costumes and gather at **Myeongnyundang (명륜당)**, the old college building. From there they march to **Gwanghwamun**. Participants sing and dance to songs created by Chung Lang during the parade, and deliver a proposal selected from a contest to an invited government official.
