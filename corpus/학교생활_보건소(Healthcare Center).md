# Healthcare Center (보건소)

To promote health and well-being of our students and faculty, the University's Health Center offers services in early detection of diseases, treatment and treatment placement, health counseling, student health checkup, etc.

##### Special Clinics

**Obesity Counseling Clinic** — To prevent and manage obesity, the clinic assesses and analyzes an individual's body composition, helps with diet and exercise plans, and offers continuous care and counseling. All obesity-related counseling and treatments are free of charge.

**Lifestyle Diseases** — Continuous care and counseling for lifestyle diseases such as high blood pressure and diabetes through detailed tests like blood pressure and blood sugar tests. Hours: Monday ~ Friday, 9:00 AM ~ 5:30 PM.

**Anxiety and Depression Counseling** — Visiting psychiatric counseling for mental stress is as natural as visiting internal medicine or surgical hospitals for physical pain. If a student is going through academic or interpersonal relationship problems because of anxiety or depression, timely and proper treatment or counseling can improve their condition significantly.

**Smoking Cessation Counseling** — SKKU offers various smoking cessation programs and education. The program schedule is announced on SKKU's notice boards.
