# IT Call Center (IT 콜센터)

Students can make requests or inquiries using SKKU's IT Call Center, which offers fast responses for internet, PC, phone, etc.

- Support sites: http://367.co.kr/ , http://6119.skku.edu/

##### Contact Info

- Phone: 031-299-6119 (ext. 6119)
- Email: callcenter@skku.edu
- Office: IT Call Center, 6F of Samsung Library, Natural Sciences Campus

##### Service Recipients and Hours

- Recipients: All IT users on campus
- Hours: Weekdays 9:00 AM ~ 5:30 PM (except holidays). IT services are provided by phone if there are major IT issues during holidays.

##### Service Areas and Range

- Areas: internet areas including websites, GLS, ASIS and My Kingo; network; PC; printer; telephone
- Range: free services for all university properties (except installations/instructions/license services for consumables, accessory equipment, commercial software, etc.)

##### Service Method

- Aims to offer immediate solutions through IT specialists and to complete service **within four hours** after receipt of an inquiry.
- If a service visit is needed, the technician makes an appointment confirmation call within ten minutes and visits at the appointed time.
- Remote control technical support is available with the user's consent.
