# Facility Utilization Instructions (시설 이용 안내)

##### Classroom (강의실 대여)

To rent a classroom for the purpose of education such as student council or club activities, please use the classroom rental application menu in GLS (visiting or calling is not possible). Students may check with the maintenance office of each classroom via the main service - classroom information menu.

- Contact: Office of Academic Support 02-760-1059 / 031-299-6648

##### Creative Design Studio (C-Office)

- Location: Creative Design Studio (33B102), Business School B1, next to the Amphitheater
- Contact: Center for Innovative Higher Education (02-740-1830~1)
- Operating Hours: Mon–Fri 9:00 AM ~ 5:30 PM (Lunch 12:00~13:00)
- Features: general team study and meeting area, laptop rental, 3D printer
- To use, apply directly to the C-Office.

##### Large Playground (대운동장)

**Humanities and Social Sciences Campus**

- Application and Approval: Every semester in February and August after an announcement on SKKU's website
- Operation: Operates autonomously on weekdays during the regular semesters and breaks if no official events are planned
- Contact: Office of Student Services (02-760-1073)

**Natural Sciences Campus**

- Sand Playground: College of Sport Science
- Artificial Turf Playground: University Varsity Team
- Contact: College of Sport Science (031-299-6920), University Varsity Team (031-299-6963)

##### Event Hall in Campus (교내 행사장)

**Humanities and Social Sciences Campus — Amphitheater / Theater**

- Application and Approval: Every semester in February and August after an announcement on SKKU's website
- Contact: Asset Management Division (02-760-1066)

**Natural Sciences Campus**

- Application Method: Visit the Office of Student Services and fill in the application form
- Contact: Office of Student Services (031-290-5036)

##### Practice Room (Sungkyun-Madang, 성균마당 연습실)

- Operating hours: 8:00 AM ~ 10:00 PM
- Location: Humanities and Social Sciences Campus, Business School B4
- Contact: Office of Student Services (02-760-1078)
- Only available to organizations composed of SKKU members.

##### Marine Living Hall (해양수련원)

- Application Period: During summer vacation
- Application Method: Application announcement in early June
- Reservations: Pay the reservation within a week after applying. Only organizations that have made reservations and paid can print out passes/receipts (bring when visiting).
- Location: Gangwon-do Samcheok
- Contact: Office of Student Services (02-760-1078)
