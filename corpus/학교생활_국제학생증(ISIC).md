# International Student ID Card / ISIC (국제학생증)

To support students' active international exchanges and activities, SKKU offers a UNESCO-certified International Student Identity Card (ISIC) service.

- Apply for ISIC: http://www.isic.co.kr/skku/
- A guide to ISIC: http://www.isic.co.kr/pack2/index.html

##### Eligibility

Undergraduates, graduates, and students on a leaves of absence are eligible for cards.
However, graduate students who have completed their courses (graduate certification completers) are not eligible.

##### How It Works

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| How to Apply | Click "Apply for Issuance of International Student ID Card" → Fill out online application → Pay issuance fee (Payment Method: specified in online application) → Visit Student Support Team on either campus |
| Issuance and Pick-up Location | The ISIC is issued by the Student Support Team at either campus. |
| Expiration Date | The ISIC is valid for a period of 12 months from the date of issuance. |
| Discount Benefits | Flights, transportation, accommodation, museums, historic sites, cultural asset tours, etc. |
