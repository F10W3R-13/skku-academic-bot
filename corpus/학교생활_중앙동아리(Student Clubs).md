# Student Clubs (중앙동아리)

> 교환학생에게 동아리를 추천할 때 쓰는 목록. 대부분 인스타그램 계정으로 직접 연락 가능합니다.
> ⚠️ 일부 동아리는 영문 페이지에 소개글 없이 계정만 등록되어 있습니다.

## 연극 · 영상 · 예술 (Theatre, Film & Art)

| Club | Instagram | Description |
|---|---|---|
| SKKU Drama Club (연극회) | @skku_dramaclub | The only central drama (acting) club since 1946; accepts anyone. Three performances a year, two of them jointly by Seoul and Suwon campuses. Any position open regardless of age or nationality. |
| neungrachon (능라촌) | @neungrachon_skku | Stages plays, studies and watches dramas. Three plays annually, entire process made by club members. |
| Cinevill | @cineville1991 | Watch movies, talk about movies, and make our own movies. Films at least one movie per semester. |
| SAPA (성균학술사진회) | @skku_sapa_1971 | The only photography club, since 1971. Weekend shoots around Seoul, weekly study sessions on each campus, long travels, photo exhibitions and contests. |
| SKKU Fine Art Club (성미회) | @skku_fine_art | For people who love drawing — mainly oils, also watercolor, acrylic, colored pencils. Exhibits works in the SKKU Library every semester. |
| greennarae (그린나래) | @greennarae_skku | Cartoon club — draws cartoons, writes stories and critiques; also animation and visual novels. Annual bulletin, weekly seminar. |
| Sung Kyun Korean Calligraphy Club (서도회) | @skku.seodo | Learns writing from Chinese characters to Korean letters. Exhibition every semester together with Suwon campus students. |
| Hangso literary society (행소문학회) | @skku.haengso | Writes poetry, novels and other literature. Gathers Friday evenings to read and critique anonymous members' writings; publishes an annual collection. |
| Paegari (패거리) | @skku_fashiongang | The only official fashion and culture club, founded 2020. Street snap shoots in Seoul, studio concept shoots each semester, publishes a fashion magazine, end-of-semester party. Open to planners, editors, photographers and models. |
| Be-Nay | @be_nay.skku | Planning and making advertisements. |
