# Certificate Issuing Services (증명서 발급)

> 📎 국문 상세 자료가 프로젝트 폴더에 이미 있습니다 → `증명서발급 및 학적부.md`. 이 문서는 **영문판**이라 교환학생 안내용으로 그대로 쓸 수 있습니다.

- Online issuance site: https://icert.skku.edu/main

## 1. Online (including Postal Service)

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Application Method | Certificate Issuance Application at https://icert.skku.edu/main |
| Issuance Period | All year round |
| Payment Method | Credit card, mobile phone |
| Required Time | Immediately |
| Notes | An electronic seal is used for all certificates issued online; they are legally valid in black-and-white or color. Certificates can be printed out and saved as files. |
| Mobile | KingoM App > About SKKU > Certificate Issue |

## 2. KIOSK

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Location | **[Humanities & Social Sciences Campus]** 2F entrance of International Hall (temporary); 1F Business School. **[Natural Sciences Campus]** 1F Engineering 2 (26th Building); 1F Student Center; B1F Dormitory Shin-gwan (B) |
| Issuance Period | All year round |
| Payment Method | Credit card, mobile phone, T-money, Cashbee, Samsung Pay |
| Required Time | Immediately |
| Notes | Log in with SKKU ID/PW, **or tag your Student ID card** (enrolled or on leave of absence) |

## 3. Official Document Services (정부24 등 관공서)

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Application Location | The official document department in province, city, county, eup, myeon and dong offices |
| Application Period | Within regular office hours |
| Payment Method | Payment at the application location |
| Required Time | Within 3 hours |

## 4. Issuance at Office (창구 발급)

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Application Location | Office of Academic Affairs — 1F, 600th Anniversary Hall (Humanities & Social Sciences Campus); 1F, Student Center (Natural Sciences Campus) |
| Office Hours | Weekdays 9:00 AM ~ 5:00 PM (closed 12:00~13:00). Closed weekends, holidays, University Anniversary Day (9/25), Confucius' Birthday (9/28) |
| Payment Method | **Cash only** |
| Required Time | Within 10 minutes |
| Note | Student's ID card is necessary — passport, SKKU student card, or Alien Registration card. A substitute may collect certificates only with (1) Substitutional Issuance Form, (2) student's ID card (copy allowed), and (3) substitute's ID card. |

## Registry Correction (name, date of birth, resident registration number)

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Office in Charge | The administration office of one's major |
| Application Method | 1. GLS → Academic Status/Personal information → Change of School Register Information. 2. Click 'ADD' in 신청내역 / 신청상세내역, choose the Type and input the data. 3. Click 'Search File' and upload proof. Updated in about 7 days; if urgent, call the administration office of your major. |
