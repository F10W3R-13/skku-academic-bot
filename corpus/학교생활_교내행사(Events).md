# Events (교내 주요 행사)

| Event | When | Department | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| University Festival (대동제) | Every year in May | Student Council | Where all Sungkyunkwan students are united and come together |
| Volunteer Activities for Rural Communities (농활) | The end of June | Student Support Team | Regular volunteer activities in the villages of Chungju City (agreement with 30 villages since 2006), held in spring, autumn, and summer |
| Walking Across the Country (국토대장정) | July | Student Council | For inspiring pride, school spirit, and patriotism based on passion and ambition |
| Overseas Culture Experience (해외문화탐방) | Announced May~June, travel July~August | Student Council | Overseas culture experience during summer vacation to foster global leadership and spread awareness of World Heritage |
| School Anniversary Festival (개교기념 축제) | September~October every year | Student Council | Culture/festival celebrating the school's anniversary; various cultural activities and events held by student council and clubs |
| Homecoming Day (입학 30·40·50주년) | September~October every year | Development and Cooperation Team | University visits, cultural performance events, campus tours, presentation of development funds, meetings by majors |
