# Services for Students with Disabilities (장애학생 지원)

##### Registration of Students with Disabilities

To offer academic and living support to students with disabilities, SKKU has a system to assist in areas such as course registration, course assistance, helper support, and scholarships.

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Registration Period | During school year |
| Required Documents | Disability registration card, government-certified, disability class certificate |
| Registration Desk | Support Center for Students with Disabilities — Humanities and Social Sciences Campus: Main Library 3F / ☎ 02-760-1092; Natural Sciences Campus: Samsung Library 3F / ☎ 031-299-4425 |
| Note | The registered information of students with disabilities is managed separately from their records in the SKKU registry and is only used for student support purposes. The information will only be registered under the students' agreement. Registration is not mandatory, but to receive support from the center, students must register their information. |

##### Scholarships — Ginkgo Tree Scholarship

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Those who were eligible and admitted to SKKU through the special admission process; those who are registered as disabled in a government institution (Class 1~4); undergraduates only |
| Payment Criteria | [Ginkgo Tree Scholarship 1] admitted through the special admission process and registered as Class 1 or 2. [Ginkgo Tree Scholarship 2] admitted through the special admission process and registered as Class 3 or 4. |
| Payment Period | Payment for the years required for graduation |
| Required Documents | Ginkgo Tree Scholarship application and a copy of disability certificate |
| Application Location | Student Support Team offices at both campuses |
| Application Period | Refer to special notices at the beginning of semesters on SKKU website |

##### Helper System for Students with Disabilities

The Helpers offer lecture notes, lecture summaries, word processing support, moving assistance, and various other support services to help students with disabilities go through learning and university life without any problems.
To apply, students must submit a Helper Assistance Request to the Support Center for Students with Disabilities within the specified time period.

##### Special Major Assignment System for Students with Disabilities

The Special Major Assignment System is designed for students with different classes due to disabilities. Inquiries: Undergraduate Office ☎ 02-760-0996.

##### Support Center and Exclusive Library Reading Room

Social workers stationed in the Support Centers for Students with Disabilities on the third floor of the Main Library and the third floor of Samsung Library offer individual students services regarding academics and general university affairs through a systematic and comprehensive One-Stop Service System.

| Facilities Available on Weekdays | During School Year (weekdays) | During Break (weekdays) | Saturdays | Sundays & Holidays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Support Center for Students with Disabilities | 9:00 AM ~ 5:00 PM | 9:00 AM ~ 5:00 PM | Closed | Closed |
| Exclusive Reading Room for Students with Disabilities | 9:00 AM ~ 9:40 PM | 9:00 AM ~ 5:00 PM | 10:00 AM ~ 5:00 PM | Closed |

Main Services: provision of learning materials; helper assistance for courses/university life; development of Braille books; assistance with course registration process; academic and job counseling support; other support and assistance for university life.

Equipment and Tools: free equipment rental including tables with electronic adjustable heights, portable electronic reading enlargers, Braille printers, screen reader software (sense readers), text recognition software (FineReader), screen magnification software (ZoomText 9.1USB), Braille notetaker (Braille Hansone), desktop computers, laptops, scanners, voice recorders, mobility scooters, wheelchairs, crutches, etc.

- Disabled Students Support Center: Main Library (3F), ☎ 02-760-1092
- Disabled Students Support Center: Samsung Library (3F), ☎ 031-299-4425

##### Main Library / Samsung Library Services

- Delivery Check-out/Return Services — On-Campus: request by phone or e-mail → delivery of the items to a specific location. Off-Campus: request by phone or e-mail → mail or parcel delivery service at student's expense. Returns follow the same process (library staff visits and picks up on campus). Please specify your contact information when making requests.
