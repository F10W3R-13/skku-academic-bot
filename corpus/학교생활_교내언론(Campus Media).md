# Campus Media (교내 언론)

SKKU offers several mass media platforms to advance students' learning, education, and public opinion through prompt delivery of news and various constructive opinions and criticisms in and out of the university.

##### SKKU Newspaper (성대신문)

"SKKU Weekly" was founded in 1954 as its first form of university media. It grew to become highly influential while going through the April Revolution of 1960. It changed its name to "SKKU Newspaper" in 1961, which continues to this day.

Currently the newspaper consists of University, Academic, Public Voice, Society, and Culture sections, and eleven issues are published each semester. Under the motto of "Progress, Future, and Hope", SKKU Newspaper aims to contribute to building a healthy student community by reflecting the students' viewpoints.

- Homepage: http://www.skkuw.com/

##### The Sungkyun Times (영자신문)

The Sungkyun Times is an English language media outlet at SKKU. It collects different ideas from the student community and provides publications in English for domestic and global readers in the University.

- Homepage: http://skt.skku.edu/

> 💡 교환학생에게 영어로 교내 소식을 안내할 때 가장 유용한 매체.

##### Sungkyunkwan University Broadcasting Studio, SUBS (교육방송국)

SUBS is the university broadcasting studio on campus that provides academic and practical information to the university community. SUBS broadcasts regular as well as special programs and hosts many different cultural events on campus. The headquarters is located at the Humanities and Social Sciences Campus, with a branch studio at the Natural Sciences Campus.

- Homepage: http://subs.skku.edu/
