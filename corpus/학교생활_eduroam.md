# eduroam (국제 무선랜 로밍)

eduroam (education roaming) is a global wireless roaming service for the international education and research community. Institutions from over 100 countries participate.

SKKU members can use their **Kingo Portal ID** when visiting other universities and institutions worldwide — without additional authentication procedures — to access those institutions' Wi-Fi.

- Domestic affiliated organizations: http://www.eduroam.kr
- Overseas affiliated organizations: http://www.eduroam.org

##### 1. SKKU members visiting other institutions

| Wi-Fi | How to use |
|---|---|
| eduroam | ① Connect to SSID 'eduroam'. ② Enter ID / PW — **ID: Kingo Portal ID + @skku.ac.kr** (e.g. aaa@skku.ac.kr). ⚠️ **@skku.ac.kr, NOT @skku.edu.** PW: Kingo Portal password. ③ Complete the per-device authentication setting. ※ Connecting to eduroam on campus before you travel makes it easier abroad. |

Inquiries: SKKU Division of Information and Communications (031-299-6119)

##### 2. Other institutions' members visiting SKKU

| Wi-Fi | How to use |
|---|---|
| eduroam | ① Connect to SSID 'eduroam' (the visitor's institution must be registered with eduroam). ② Enter the ID and password used at their own institution. ③ Complete the per-device authentication setting following their own institution's guide (not SKKU's). |

Inquiries: the wireless network division of the visitor's own institution.
